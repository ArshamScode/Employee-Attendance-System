HOW TO GET STARTED:
1. LAUNCH: Double-click the "Employee Attendance System" icon on your desktop.
2. FIRST TIME RUN: Windows may ask for permission to access your camera. Click "Allow".
3. ADD EMPLOYEES: 
   - Click the "Employees" tab (Default username: AS_admin, Default password: AS_P@ssword-Emp)
   - Click "Add", enter employee details, and take a photo.
   - Repeat for all employees.
4. TRAIN THE SYSTEM: 
   - On the main "Attendance" page, click the "Train Model" button.
   - The system will learn the faces of all added employees.
5. START SCANNING: 
   - Employees can now stand in front of the camera to automatically log their Entry and Exit times.
